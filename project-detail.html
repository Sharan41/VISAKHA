Project Details

A home built around its courtyard

The Courtyard Residence sits on a 2,400 sq ft east-facing plot in Anna Nagar, Chennai. Every room in the 3,450 sq ft plan opens onto a planted internal courtyard, so light and cross-ventilation reach the centre of the house without opening it up to the street. The ground floor holds the living, dining, kitchen, pooja and a guest bedroom around that court; three bedrooms and a family lounge sit above.
